# Fresh Ultima VII reference study

The latest user request explicitly authorizes starting the 64×64 terrain from
scratch, following the supplied Ultima VII layout, without a town wall. This
supersedes the earlier Minoc terrain-preservation and perimeter instructions for
this experiment only. Never overwrite maps/Minoc.json or the earlier study.
Preserve NPC, conversation, sign, contents and type/artwork metadata. Record and
report old schedules/quest positions that no longer fit the fresh terrain; do not
silently relocate or delete them. This is a new layout study, not a play-tested
replacement for the established game world.

Central and southeast buildings are factories; the southwest waterfront building
is the shipbuilder. Preserve the reference's southwest bay, northern river bend
around the southeast factory, short western branch and eastern outflow south.
There is no town wall or gate. Use the bottom-center arrival at (32,62).
Worker dormitories use rough stone wall 0x4d with rows of paired 0xab/0xac beds and
clear aisles. Ordinary outdoors must not contain interior floor tiles. Timber
flooring outside a building is allowed only in explicitly mapped shipyard piers.
Use directional road and river artwork; inspect every new preview and close-up.
